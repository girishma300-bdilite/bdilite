01 — Company

A technology company built around dependable delivery.

BdiLite Technologies is a software engineering company headquartered in Bengaluru, India. We help organizations modernize how they build and run software — from customer-facing web platforms to the cloud infrastructure and automation behind them.

Our teams work across product engineering, cloud and DevOps, and systems integration. We take accountability for the full lifecycle: design, build, security, and ongoing operations.

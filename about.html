About BdiLite Technologies

A software engineering company based in Bengaluru, India, helping businesses build and operate reliable software.

BdiLite Technologies is a software engineering company headquartered in Koramangala, Bengaluru. We work with growing businesses and enterprises to design, build, and operate the systems their teams and customers rely on.

Our work spans four connected practices — web and product engineering, cloud and DevOps, automation, and API integration. Rather than handing off code and walking away, we take responsibility for the full lifecycle, from architecture through to monitoring and support in production.

We are a product- and outcome-focused company. Every engagement starts with the problem the business is trying to solve, and is measured by whether the software delivers on it.
